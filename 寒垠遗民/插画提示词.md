# 《寒垠遗民》插画提示词

> 作者：jwk000
> 用途：把本文件里的提示词逐条复制到任意绘图模型，生成封面与 51 章插画。
> 全书共 5 卷 51 章，另附封面 4 版、卷首图 5 张。

---

## 一、怎么用

1. **每条提示词前面，先拼上「二、通用风格块」那一整段。** 这是全书调子统一的关键，别省。
2. 卷二、卷三用「灰星调」风格块，卷四、卷五换成「地球调」风格块。
3. 提示词里的**人物外形每次都重复写了一遍**，这是故意的——模型不会记住上一张图长什么样。别删。
4. 建议尺寸：封面 `2:3` 竖幅（如 1024×1536）；章首插画 `3:4` 或 `4:3` 都可以，同一批统一即可。
5. 一条提示词先出 4 张挑 1 张。挑中的那张留下来当风格参考图，后面几条带上它，人物会更稳。
6. 如果模型只吃英文，用每条下面的 **English** 那行，风格块也用英文版。

### 负面提示词（每条都加上）

```
文字, 水印, 署名, logo, 边框, 卡通, 动漫, 赛博朋克霓虹, 蒸汽朋克, 华丽奇幻,
高饱和, 粉紫色调, 塑料感, 过度磨皮, 现代都市, 智能手机, 现代服饰
```

```
text, watermark, signature, logo, frame, cartoon, anime, cyberpunk neon,
steampunk, high saturation, plastic look, modern city, smartphone, modern clothing
```

### 两条硬规矩

- **卷一至卷三，画面里不要出现海，也不要出现大面积蓝色水体。** 全书前三分之二，「海」只活在梦里、歌里和一张照片上。这条是原作的核心设定，画出来就俗了。卷一至卷三唯一允许的蓝色，是梦、屏幕、和那张照片。
- **灰星没有树、没有草、没有大型动物。** 地面上只有冻土、冰、盐漠和矮小的灌木。活物都在地下。

```
天空是铅灰色到铁锈色，没有蓝天白云，日照微弱，一切都像曝光不足。

人物穿厚重分层的旧衣物，护目镜、面罩、绑腿、毛毡，颜色脏而旧。
没有文字，没有水印，没有 logo。
```

**English**

```
cinematic realistic illustration, cold restrained sci-fi mood, low-saturation grey palette,
35mm film grain, natural light, shallow depth of field, wide negative space,
tiny human figures against vast environments, overcast leaden-to-rust coloured sky,
no blue sky, weak sunlight, underexposed feel, only grey brown rust and dirty white,
the only warm colour is fire, the only vivid colour is blood,
people in heavy layered worn clothing, goggles, masks, leg wraps, felt,
no text, no watermark, no logo
```

```
people in remade worn clothing faded by washing,
no text, no watermark, no logo
```

## 四、封面提示词

封面建议竖幅 2:3。下面四版任选，风格块照抄不留。

### 封面 A｜极简版（推荐做正式封面）

**中文**

```
一张文学小说封面。画面极简：画面上方三分之二是一整片铅灰色的天空，
由近黑渐变到冷灰，看不见太阳，有一条极细的雨丝纹理；
画面下方三分之一是一道笔直的、微微发亮的地平线，
地平线以下是一片深蓝色的、安静的、看不见波纹的水面，
水面上方有一层淡淡的光晕。画面正中留出大片空白，
不画人，不画船，不画任何具体物体。整体冷静、克制、辽阔、有一点悲意。
竖幅 2:3 构图，胶片颗粒，留白极多。
```

**English**

```
a literary novel cover, ultra-minimal. Top two thirds is a single leaden grey sky,
gradient from near-black to cold grey, no sun visible, faint vertical rain texture.
Bottom third is a perfectly straight faintly glowing horizon line,
below it a deep blue, still, rippleless expanse of water with a soft glow just under the line.
Large empty space in the centre, no people, no ship, no objects at all.
Cold, restrained, vast, faintly sorrowful.
vertical 2:3 composition, film grain, lots of negative space
```

## 六、卷一至卷三　分章插画

> 风格块一律用「2.1 灰星调」。

### 第 01 章　雨季的第一天

**中文**
灰星地表，雨季的第一天。地平线上浮起一层白雾——不是雪也不是沙，是水汽。几百个穿着厚重旧衣的人从一个地下窖口爬出来，站在一片空地上，仰着头，张着嘴，用脸接雨。前景偏左是一座锈蚀的铁哨塔，塔上站着一个二十出头的年轻士兵，戴护目镜，往下看。远处天边有三点极小的炮火闪光。铅灰色天，细密的雨丝。构图：人物压在画面下方，天空占大半。

**English**
surface of a grey planet, first day of the rainy season. A band of white mist rising off the horizon — not snow, not sand, water vapour. Several hundred people in heavy worn clothes climbing out of a cellar mouth, standing in the open, heads back, mouths open, catching rain on their faces. Foreground left: a rusted iron watchtower with a young soldier in his early twenties on top, goggles on, looking down. Three tiny muzzle flashes on the far horizon. Leaden sky, fine rain. Figures low in frame, sky filling most of it.

### 第 02 章　三十天的命

**中文**
雨季里的聚居地。俯视：一片低矮的地窖口、浅坑、引水的石渠、一小块一小块刚冒头的矮麦田。几十个人在泥里弯腰干活，有人挑水，有人修渠，有人在远处挖坑埋人。所有东西都是灰的、褐的、锈红的，只有刚长出来的矮麦是极淡的一点黄绿。天是铅灰色的，还在下小雨。

**English**
a settlement during the rainy season. Aerial view: low cellar mouths, shallow pits, stone water channels, small patches of barely-sprouted dwarf wheat. Dozens of people bent over working in mud — carrying water, repairing a channel, and far off, digging a grave. Everything grey, brown, rust red, except one faint yellow-green of new wheat. Leaden sky, still drizzling.

### 第 03 章　闭月

**中文**
雨季最后一夜，气温在几十小时里骤降六十度。画面是一片结满白霜的空地，空气中悬着冰晶，能见度很低。一个瘦小的少年兵缩在一块挡风的铁板后，把两只手揣在怀里，脸冻得发青，睫毛上全是霜。旁边一桶水正在从表面开始结冰。远处的灯是唯一一点暖色。极度寒冷，画面几乎是白与灰的。

**English**
the last night of the rainy season, temperature dropping sixty degrees in a few dozen hours. A field of white frost, ice crystals hanging in the air, visibility very low. A small skinny teenage soldier curled behind a sheet of iron used as a windbreak, both hands tucked into his coat, face blue with cold, frost on his lashes. A bucket of water beside him freezing from the surface down. One distant lamp is the only warm point. Extreme cold, the image almost entirely white and grey.

### 第 04 章　七号水源

**中文**
寒季的巡逻。一行五六个裹得严严实实的人走在冻硬的荒地上，脚印很浅。前景是一个年轻士兵握着枪，枪口朝下，指节发白，脸上是第一次开过枪之后的表情——不是害怕，是发懵。雪地上有一小片被踩乱的痕迹。远处是被冻住的、灰白色的山脊。没有太阳。

**English**
a patrol in the cold season. Five or six people wrapped to the eyes walking across hard-frozen waste, footprints shallow. Foreground: a young soldier gripping a rifle, muzzle down, knuckles white, wearing the face of someone who has just fired a gun for the first time — not fear, blankness. A small patch of trampled disturbance in the snow. Behind, frozen grey-white ridges. No sun.

### 第 05 章　铁刺

**中文**
盐漠边缘。一丛丛像铁钉一样立着的、灰黑色的刺状灌木，风把它们吹得发响。画面中间是一次伏击之后的静止：一辆用废铁焊的运货车歪在一边，地上有拖拽的痕迹。前景是一双被打穿的大腿，旁边有人用布死死按住伤口。远处刺丛上挂着一件衣服，没有人去取。灰白盐碱地，低压的铅灰天空。

**English**
edge of a salt desert. Clumps of grey-black spike-like shrubs standing up like nails, wind making them rasp. Centre: the stillness after an ambush — a scrap-welded cargo cart tipped on its side, drag marks on the ground. Foreground: a leg shot through, hands pressing cloth hard over the wound. Far off, a piece of clothing hanging on the thorns, nobody going to get it. Grey-white salt flat, low leaden sky.

### 第 06 章　三湖之战

**中文**
三十小时之后的三湖。泥、冰、血混在一起，分不清哪是哪。画面里横着几具尸体，半个身子冻在冰里。前景偏右是一个跪在冰上的年轻士兵，怀里抱着一个十六岁少年的身体，少年的背上有洞，脸朝下。士兵没有哭，只是低着头。远处还有人在打，有火光。整体是灰、白、暗红三种颜色。

**English**
the three lakes after thirty hours. Mud, ice and blood mixed until you cannot tell them apart. Several bodies lying across the frame, half frozen into the ice. Foreground right: a young soldier kneeling on the ice holding the body of a sixteen-year-old boy, a hole in the boy's back, face down. The soldier is not crying, only looking down. Far off the fighting is still going on, with firelight. Only three colours: grey, white, dark red.

### 第 07 章　坟场

**中文**
盐漠深处，十七艘八百年前的飞船残骸散落成一大片。它们有一半埋在盐里，外壳锈成暗褐色，形状还看得出是船。画面近处：一个年轻士兵站在一扇圆窗的边上，手里拿着一根钉子，正在金属边缘上一笔一笔地刻一个字。他刻得很慢。远景里还有几十个极小的人在拆钢板。天色将晚，冷白色的光。

**English**
deep in a salt desert, seventeen starship wrecks from eight hundred years ago scattered over a wide area. Half buried in salt, hulls rusted dark brown, still recognisable as ships. Near foreground: a young soldier standing beside a circular window, a single nail in his hand, slowly carving one character into the metal edge. Dozens of tiny figures stripping plates further off. Dusk, cold white light.

### 第 08 章　红雨

**中文**
灰星地表，天上的雨是铁锈色的，浑浊，落在雪上留下一片片红褐色的斑。地上成片枯死的矮小麦和苔藓，全部倒伏。远处聚居地的烟囱没有冒烟。画面中间有一个极小的人影，站着不动，抬头看天。整个世界像被泡在铁锈水里。压抑，没有生机。

**English**
grey planet surface. The rain is rust-coloured, muddy, leaving red-brown stains across the snow. Fields of dead dwarf wheat and moss lying flat. No smoke from the settlement chimneys far off. One tiny figure standing motionless in the middle of the frame, looking up at the sky. The whole world looks soaked in rust water. Oppressive, lifeless.

### 第 09 章　大寒

**中文**

### 第 13 章　森

**English**

**English**

### 第 17 章　最后一战

**English**
inside the frame of an unfinished ship, a battle just ended. Bodies between the enormous metal ribs, welding gear still smoking, blood and metal filings on the floor. Grey and dark red.

### 第 18 章　拣选

### 第 19 章　告别

## 七、卷四、卷五　分章插画

> 从这里开始，风格块换成「2.2 地球调」。人物衣着也换了：不再是灰星的厚重旧衣，而是拆改过的、洗得发浅的衣服。

### 第 24 章　落地

**中文**
一片湿地上的清晨。一艘烧得焦黑、变形的旧船歪在浅水里，还在冒一丝烟。前景是一个年轻士兵站在泥地里，刚把头盔从头上摘下来，抱在怀里，仰着头，闭着眼，脸上有水——雨还是泪分不清。他张着嘴，在呼吸。四周是雾、绿草和远处的树线。暖白的晨光。

**English**

Foreground: a young soldier standing in the mud, helmet just off his head and held to his chest, head back, eyes closed, face wet — rain or tears, unclear. His mouth is open. He is breathing. Around him fog, green grass, a treeline in the distance. Warm white morning light.

### 第 25 章　绿

**中文**
一片湿润的、深绿色的树林。一群穿着旧衣服的成年人站在树下，像小孩一样乱跑，有人把整个脸贴在树干上，有人伸手去够叶子，有人蹲在地上摸苔。他们的动作很笨。光线从树叶间落下来，是湿的、暖白的。画面里的绿色多到让人不习惯。

**English**

A group of adults in worn clothes standing under the trees, running about like children — one pressing his whole face against a tree trunk, one reaching up for leaves, one crouching to touch the moss. ### 第 26 章　咸的

**中文**

**English**
a beach. A man stands at the water's edge, shoes off, one foot just set into the water, a ring of white foam across the top of his foot. His body is rigid, as if afraid to move. ### 第 27 章　第一年

**中文**

**English**
on a gentle slope by a river mouth, the first houses going up. Built from plates stripped off the ship and rebar salvaged from ruins, low and shapeless. Children walking in the mud, a woman holding a baby. Behind, the old ship half dismantled, its bow pointing inland. Bright sky, clouds. Messy, but alive.

### 第 28 章　睡不着的人

**中文**
夜里的新村子。几间低矮的屋子亮着一点点灯。村子外面是一个刚立起来的木牌，上面刻着许多道横线，一道一道，很密。一个老人蹲在牌子前面，手里拿着刀在刻新的一道。月亮照在草地上。安静，克制，几乎看不出情绪。

**English**
the new village at night. A few low houses with small lights. Outside the village, a newly raised wooden board covered in many short carved strokes, one after another, tightly packed. An old man crouches in front of it, knife in hand, cutting a new one. Moonlight on the grass. Quiet, restrained, almost no visible emotion.

### 第 29 章　遗迹

**中文**
内陆的荒地上，草丛里露出一角混凝土和一根弯掉的钢筋，旁边斜插着一块金属牌，字已经被锈吃掉。几个人蹲在旁边看，其中一个人伸手把草拨开。周围是两米厚的、深色的新土层。光线是亮的、干的。谁都不说话。

**English**
inland wasteland: a corner of concrete and one bent piece of rebar showing through the grass, and beside it a metal sign leaning over, its lettering eaten away by rust. ### 第 30 章　城

### 第 31 章　字

### 第 40 章　它醒了

**中文**
夜里的崖顶。整堵板墙的指示灯一齐亮起来，冷青色，照亮了周围的草。画面里没有人——只有一排被照亮的孩子，站在墙前面，仰着头。海在下面。这一刻是安静的，不是可怕的。

**English**
the top of the cliff at night. Every indicator light on the whole wall comes on at once, cold cyan, lighting the grass around it. No adults in the frame — only a row of lit children standing before the wall, heads back. The sea below. The moment is quiet, not frightening.

### 第 41 章　船

**中文**
海边，一条刚造好的小船停在沙滩上，是用拆下来的板和废钢筋钉的，形状很拙。一个二十岁的姑娘站在船边，一只手搭在船帮上，正在说什么。海水刚漫到船尾。清晨，雾还没散。这是他们在这儿造的第一条船。

**English**
by the sea, a newly built small boat resting on the sand, nailed together from salvaged plates and scrap rebar, its shape clumsy. A young woman of twenty stands at its side, one hand on the gunwale, mid-sentence. Water just reaching the stern. Early morning, fog not yet lifted. It is the first boat they have built here.

### 第 42 章　大的东西

**中文**
海面上，一条小船，船上有五六个人。水下面有一个巨大的、模糊的、缓慢移动的阴影，比整条船大得多，看不清是什么。所有人都趴在船边往下看。海面平静，天是亮的。画面里没有人害怕，只有好奇。

**English**
a small boat on the sea with five or six people aboard. Below the surface a huge, blurred shape moving slowly, far larger than the boat, impossible to identify. Everyone is leaning over the side looking down. The sea is calm, the sky bright. Nobody is frightened, only curious.

### 第 43 章　种子

**中文**
一片田。近处是一小把装在布袋里的种子，只有一小捧，是画面里唯一的一点黄白色。田里的矮麦长着，穗子在风里。一只手伸进画面，捏着一穗，正在看。远处是海。光很足。

**English**
a field. Near the front a small cloth bag of seed, only a handful, the one pale yellow-white thing in the picture. Dwarf wheat growing in the field, ears moving in the wind. A hand reaches into frame holding one ear, examining it. The sea beyond. Plenty of light.

### 第 44 章　灰色

**中文**
一个九十岁的老人睡着了，脸上很安静，嘴角有一点松。窗外是绿树和亮的下午。画面边缘有一点虚化，暗示这是一场梦——梦里能看到什么，画面里不给。只有老人和窗外的绿色。

**English**
a ninety-year-old man asleep, his face calm, a faint slackness at the mouth. Outside the window, green trees and bright afternoon. A little blur at the edges of the frame to suggest a dream — what is in the dream is not shown. Only the old man, and the green outside.

### 第 45 章　有东西了

**中文**
崖顶的夜空，板墙的指示灯亮着，指向天上某个方向。几个大人站在墙前，其中一个抬着手，指着天上一个点。天上是一个很干净的星空，有一条淡淡的银河。海在下面黑着。安静，漫长。

**English**
the night sky above the cliff, the wall of panels lit and aimed at some point in the sky. A few adults stand before it, one with an arm raised pointing at a single point. Above, a very clean starfield with a faint band of galaxy. The sea black below. Quiet, and long.

### 第 46 章　灰星来的话

**中文**
村子中间那个立着的金属圆环下面，围了一村子的人。圆环旁边的扬声器在响，所有人都在听，有人捂着嘴，有人蹲下了。一个老人站在最前面，抬着头。中午，光是亮的，画面却很静。

**English**
beneath the upright metal circle in the middle of the village, the whole village gathered. A loudspeaker beside it is sounding; everyone listening, one person with a hand over her mouth, one crouched down. An old man stands at the front, head up. Midday, bright light, and yet the image is very still.

### 第 47 章　二十三年一回

**中文**
雨季的第一天，崖顶。一排人站成一排，一个一个地对着板墙说话。他们身后是海，天上下着很细的雨。每个人手里都拿着一小片纸。像某种仪式，但没有人穿得正式。光很柔。

**English**
the first day of the rainy season, on top of the cliff. A row of people standing in a line, each speaking to the wall of panels in turn. Behind them the sea; fine rain falling. Each holds a small scrap of paper. It is a ritual, though nobody is dressed for one. Soft light.

### 第 48 章　回话

**中文**
村子中间空地上，人们围着一圈坐着，中间站着一个人在说话，双手摊开。没有桌子，没有椅子，像是在开会又像是在商量一件家事。天色是傍晚。地上有孩子画的圈。

**English**
in the open ground in the middle of the village, people sitting in a circle, one person standing in the middle talking, hands open. No table, no chairs — it looks like a meeting and like a family argument. Evening. Chalk circles drawn on the ground by children.

### 第 49 章　平常

**中文**
村子中间那个金属圆环旁边，几个小孩把它当成门，一趟一趟地钻过来钻过去，一边钻一边在说笑。一个很老的老人坐在旁边的石头上看着他们，手里拿着一个本子。海在远处。下午的光很亮。孩子们对这一切熟得不能再熟。

**English**
beside the upright metal circle in the middle of the village, several children are using it as a doorway, running through it again and again, laughing as they go. A very old man sits on a stone nearby watching them, a notebook in his hand. The sea in the distance. Bright afternoon light. The children find all of this completely ordinary.

### 第 50 章　交接

**中文**
傍晚，屋外。一个很老的老人把一本写满了的本子递出去，递到一个二十四岁的年轻人手里。年轻人的手接住了，两个人的手都停在半空。旁边没有人。背景是低矮的屋子和远处的海。安静，克制。

**English**
dusk, outside. A very old man holds out a filled notebook to a young man of twenty-four. The young man's hands have closed on it; both pairs of hands are still in the air. Nobody else around. Low houses behind, the sea far off. Quiet, restrained.

### 第 51 章　铭牌

**中文**
海边，清晨。一个老人蹲在水边，把三块很小的金属铭牌一块一块地放进水里，水面刚好漫过它们。他的手在水里停着。身后沙滩上站着一群孩子，在看着。海是蓝的，一直在响。晨光很淡。这是最后一张图。

**English**
by the sea, early morning. An old man crouching at the water's edge, setting three small metal name-plates into the water one at a time until it just covers them. His hand stays in the water. Behind him on the sand, a group of children watching. The sea is blue, and it keeps sounding. Faint morning light. This is the last image.

## 八、几个实用建议

**关于一致性**
- 先只画第一章和第一章的人物。挑出最满意的一张，当作「风格参考图」喂给后面所有提示词。
- 每条的英文版里都重复了人物外形，别嫌啰嗦，这是唯一能让第 3 张和第 30 张像同一个人的办法。
- 一次性出全套容易崩，建议按卷来：先把卷一 7 张画顺了，再开卷二。

**关于顺序**
- 想省事，可以先画这 8 张定调子：封面 A、卷四《绿》、卷五《归》、第 06 章、第 13 章、第 26 章、第 32 章、第 51 章。这 8 张过了，剩下的都照它们的调子走。

**备选方向**（如果觉得写实风太常见）
- **木刻版画风**：黑、灰、一点暗红，粗线条，强烈的刀味，适合灰星。把风格块换成 `黑白木刻版画，粗粝的刀痕，大面积黑，只有血是暗红`。
- **水墨风**：只在卷四、卷五用，湿润的墨点，淡青色，适合表现「地球自己好了」。`宣纸质感，淡墨，青色点染，大量留白`。

**别忘了**
- 卷一至卷三不要出现海和大面积蓝水。
- 灰星不要出现树和草。
- 全书不要出现现代都市、智能手机、现代服装——这个故事里没有这些。
